3. Controlling for Seniority Confounding (Query #8)

          

            The Interviewer Question: "Could the 70% longer tenure just be Simpson's Paradox—because senior employees (L3/L4) naturally have longer tenure AND get more bonuses?"
            


            How Our SQL Answers It: Query #8 stratifies the cohort by job_level (L1 through L4). Within every single level, bonus recipients still have +52% to +75% longer tenure:

            Statistical Honesty Note for Interviews: Even after controlling for job level, observational payroll data can include reverse causality (employees who stay longer have more review cycles to earn bonuses). State clearly in interviews that this is a strong observational association (+69.91%) that motivates targeted retention bonus experiments.

              "I designed a 6-table normalized MySQL/SQLite payroll schema covering 180 employees and 1,019 monthly statements. Using CTEs, window functions (RANK() OVER), and conditional aggregations, I reconciled gross-to-net compensation across base service hours, tiered overtime (1.5x/1.75x), progressive Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) slabs (21.22% effective withholding), and non-taxable expense reimbursements with zero cent-level drift.
